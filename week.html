This Week

Spurgeon wrote one morning piece and one evening piece for every calendar day. This page shows the class week of Monday 5 October through Sunday 11 October 2026. Click a morning or evening title to open that full body on the Reading page.

Today shows the pair for the local date when it falls in this week. The table below lists each day's title and Scripture reference.

How to read the table

Start on the left with the weekday. Then read across for that day's morning title and evening title. The line under each title is the Scripture reference Spurgeon printed with the piece. Each title is a link to the matching full reading.
