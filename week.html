This Week

Spurgeon wrote one morning piece and one evening piece for every calendar day. This page shows a sample week of titles from that year-round plan: 13 August through 19 August. The class feed dated those rows 2026-08-13 through 2026-08-19 so the table can sit next to the Home sample date.

Each cell keeps the published title and, at most, one short opening line. Full bodies stay off this public site. For a longer Home sample, see today’s featured readings.

Sample week titles

Morning and evening titles for 13 to 19 August. Public-domain text from C. H. Spurgeon, Morning and Evening.
Day Morning title Evening title
Thursday 13 August The Cedars of Lebanon

“The cedars of Lebanon which he hath planted.”

 I Will Remember My Covenant

“And I will remember my covenant.”
Friday 14 August Glad Through Thy Work

“Thou, Lord, hast made me glad through thy work.”

 I Know Their Sorrows

“I know their sorrows.”
Saturday 15 August Isaac Went Out to Meditate

“Isaac went out to meditate in the field at the eventide.”

 A Heart of Flesh

“And I will give you an heart of flesh.”
Sunday 16 August The Glory Due unto His Name

“Give unto the Lord the glory due unto his name.”

 The Firstfruits of the Spirit

“Ourselves also, which have the firstfruits of the Spirit.”
Monday 17 August The Mercy of God

“The mercy of God.”

 This Sickness Is Not unto Death

“This sickness is not unto death.”
Tuesday 18 August Strangers in the Sanctuaries

“Strangers are come into the sanctuaries of the Lord’s house.”

 He Received It Not

“And they gave him to drink wine mingled with myrrh: but he received it not.”
Wednesday 19 August He Shall Stand and Feed

“He shall stand and feed in the strength of the Lord.”

 Pull Me Out of the Net

“Pull me out of the net that they have laid privily for me: for thou art my strength.”

How to read the table

Start on the left with the weekday. Then read across for that day’s morning title and evening title. The one-line excerpt is the opening Scripture sentence Spurgeon printed with the piece, not the full meditation.

The week is a sample, not a live calendar feed. Titles come from the class vault-feed file this-week.json. Rights: public domain via CCEL’s Morning and Evening.
